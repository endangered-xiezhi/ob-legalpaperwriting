## 一、知识产权法总论

### 基础概念

- intellectual property：知识产权。
  - 例句：The startup's intellectual property included patents, software copyright and several registered trademarks.
  - 常用缩写：IP。
  - 口头表达：Intellectual property protects certain intangible results of human creativity and business activity.
  - 中文提示：知识产权保护特定的智力创造成果和经营性标志等无形成果。
- intellectual property right：知识产权权利。
  - 例句：A valid intellectual property right may allow its owner to stop specified unauthorized commercial acts.
  - 常用缩写：IP right 或 IPR。
- intangible asset：无形资产。
  - 例句：The trademark became the company's most valuable intangible asset after years of advertising.
  - 口头表达：An IP right is intangible, but it may have substantial commercial value.
- subject matter：保护客体。
  - 例句：The court first asked whether the generated image was eligible[有资格的有能力的] subject matter under copyright law.
  - 口头表达：The first question is whether the subject matter is eligible for protection.
- right holder：权利人。
  - 例句：Only the right holder or an authorized party could grant the streaming licence[流媒体许可证/网络视听节目许可证].
  - 同义表达：rights owner。
- exclusive right：专有权、排他权。
  - 例句：The patent gave its owner an exclusive right to manufacture and sell the claimed device.
  - 口头表达：An exclusive right allows the right holder to prevent certain unauthorized acts.
- territoriality：地域性。
  - 例句：Because of territoriality, a Chinese patent did not automatically prohibit manufacture in another country.
  - 口头表达：IP rights are territorial and normally operate within the granting state.
- term of protection：保护期。
  - 例句：The term of protection ended before the defendant began reproducing the old novel.
- public domain：公有领域。
  - 例句：The original symphony was in the public domain, although the new recording had separate protection.
  - 口头表达：After the term expires, the protected subject matter generally enters the public domain.
- public interest：公共利益。
  - 例句：Public interest weighed against stopping access to an essential medicine during an emergency.
- balance of interests：利益平衡。
  - 例句：The exception reflected a balance of interests between authors and classroom users.
  - 口头表达：IP law seeks to balance private incentives and the public interest.
- incentive to create：创作激励。
  - 例句：Copyright offers an incentive to create by allowing authors to control important uses of their works.
- incentive to innovate：创新激励。
  - 例句：Patent disclosure trades a temporary monopoly for an incentive to innovate and share technical knowledge.
- unfair competition：不正当竞争。
  - 例句：Copying the package design could constitute unfair competition even if no patent was infringed.
- international treaty：国际条约。
  - 例句：The court consulted an international treaty when interpreting protection for foreign authors.
- national treatment：国民待遇。
  - 例句：National treatment required foreign authors to receive protection comparable to domestic authors.
- minimum standards of protection：最低保护标准。
  - 例句：TRIPS requires members to provide minimum standards of protection while allowing implementation choices.
- TRIPS Agreement：与贸易有关的知识产权协定。
  - 例句：The TRIPS Agreement links international trade rules with minimum obligations for intellectual property protection.
  - 口头表达：The TRIPS Agreement sets minimum standards for IP protection among WTO members.

### 权利分析通用动词

- protect：保护。
  - 例句：The statute may protect an original photograph even when the photograph has limited commercial value.
- grant：授予权利。
  - 例句：The patent office may grant a patent only after the application satisfies the statutory requirements.
- acquire：取得权利。
  - 例句：An applicant may acquire trademark rights through registration under the applicable legal system.
- own：享有、拥有权利。
  - 例句：An employee may create a work, while the employer may own some or all of the economic rights.
- exercise a right：行使权利。
  - 例句：A right holder must exercise a right within the limits imposed by law and public interest.
- license：许可。
  - 例句：The copyright owner may license a streaming platform to make the film available online.
- assign：转让。
  - 例句：The inventor may assign the patent application to the company under a written agreement.
- authorize：授权、许可实施。
  - 例句：The trademark owner authorized the distributor to use the mark on genuine goods.
- prohibit：禁止。
  - 例句：A patent allows its owner to prohibit unauthorized manufacture and sale of the claimed product.
- infringe：侵犯。
  - 例句：A seller may infringe a trademark by using a confusingly similar sign on identical goods.
- enforce：执行、实施权利。
  - 例句：The right holder may enforce the patent by seeking an injunction and damages.
- revoke：撤销。
  - 例句：The authority may revoke a right when a statutory ground for revocation is established.
- invalidate：宣告无效。
  - 例句：A court or competent authority may invalidate a patent that lacks novelty.
- expire：期满失效。
  - 例句：When the patent expires, competitors may normally use the disclosed invention without permission.

### 口试高频区分

- ownership：所有权或权利归属。
  - 例句：The photographer proved ownership by producing the original files and the written assignment.
  - 强调谁享有权利。
- authorship：作者身份。
  - 例句：Authorship depended on who made the creative choices, not who merely financed the project.
  - 强调谁进行了创作。
- entitlement：取得权利的资格或权源。
  - 例句：The employment agreement determined the company's entitlement to apply for the patent.
  - 强调某人为何有权取得权利。
- infringement：侵权。
  - 例句：Even a valid patent is not infringed unless the accused product falls within the claims.
  - 强调未经许可实施受控制行为。
- invalidity：权利无效。
  - 例句：The defendant raised invalidity by showing that every claim element appeared in an earlier publication.
  - 强调权利本身不应成立或继续存在。
- 记忆句：Ownership, validity and infringement are separate questions and should be analyzed in that order.

## 二、著作权法：客体与保护条件

### 基础术语

- copyright：版权、著作权。
  - 例句：Copyright prevented the publisher from reproducing the novel without the author's permission.
- author's right：作者权。
  - 例句：The concept of author's right emphasizes the personal connection between the creator and the work.
  - 口头表达：The copyright tradition emphasizes economic control, while the author's-right tradition gives greater weight to the personal link between the author and the work.
- work：作品。
  - 例句：The court treated the short film as a work because it contained original audiovisual expression.
- author：作者。
  - 例句：The person who selected the camera angle, lighting and composition was the author of the photograph.
- originality：独创性。
  - 例句：The portrait satisfied originality because the photographer independently made creative visual choices.
  - 发音提示：originality 的重音在第三音节附近。
  - 口头表达：Originality requires independent creation and a minimum degree of creativity.
- independent creation：独立创作。
  - 例句：Independent creation defeated copying even though the two songs happened to sound similar.
- creativity：创造性、创作性。
  - 例句：Alphabetical ordering showed too little creativity to support protection for the directory's arrangement.
- expression：表达。
  - 例句：The novel's specific dialogue was protected expression, unlike its general time-travel idea.
- idea：思想、观念。
  - 例句：The idea of a detective solving a locked-room mystery remained free for other writers to use.
- idea-expression dichotomy：思想与表达二分法。
  - 例句：The idea-expression dichotomy protected the game's artwork but not its abstract rules.
  - 口头表达：Copyright protects expression rather than ideas, methods or principles.
- fixation：固定。
  - 例句：Recording the improvised dance in a video supplied the fixation needed for lasting identification.
  - 口头表达：Fixation means that the expression is recorded in a sufficiently stable form.
- tangible medium：有形载体。
  - 例句：The melody was fixed in a tangible medium when it was saved as a digital audio file.
- literary work：文字作品。
  - 例句：The court classified the original novel and its written dialogue as a literary work.
- artistic work：美术作品。
  - 例句：The claimant argued that the original painting was an artistic work protected against reproduction.
- musical work：音乐作品。
  - 例句：The melody and harmony formed the musical work, separately from the later sound recording.
- dramatic work：戏剧作品。
  - 例句：The script, dialogue and stage directions together constituted a dramatic work.
- audiovisual work：视听作品。
  - 例句：The continuous sequence of moving images and sound qualified as an audiovisual work.
- photographic work：摄影作品。
  - 例句：Creative choices in framing, light and timing made the portrait a photographic work.
- computer program：计算机程序。
  - 例句：The developer claimed copyright in the source code of the computer program, not in its general function.
- compilation：汇编作品。
  - 例句：The database qualified as a compilation because its selection and arrangement reflected creative choices.
- derivative work：演绎作品。
  - 例句：The unauthorized film adaptation was a derivative work based on the earlier novel.
- work of applied art：实用艺术作品。
  - 例句：The decorative shape of the lamp could qualify as a work of applied art apart from its lighting function.
- functional feature：功能性特征。
  - 例句：The location of the command button was a functional feature dictated by efficient operation.
- method of operation：操作方法。
  - 例句：The menu commands were treated as a method of operation needed to control the software.
- merger doctrine：混同原则。
  - 例句：The merger doctrine limited protection because the rule could be expressed in only a few practical ways.
  - 口头表达：When an idea can be expressed only in a very limited number of ways, the expression may merge with the idea.
- scènes à faire：必然场景、标准场景。
  - 例句：Police stations and crime-scene tape were scènes à faire in a conventional detective drama.
  - 发音可近似记为：sen-ah-fair。
  - 口头表达：Standard elements dictated by a theme or setting are generally not protected.

### 人工智能生成内容

- AI-generated content：人工智能生成内容。
  - 例句：The AI-generated content was not automatically attributed to the user who entered a single short prompt.
- human authorship：人类作者身份。
  - 例句：Human authorship was doubtful because the system, rather than the user, determined the final visual details.
- prompt：提示词。
  - 例句：The prompt described a sunset but did not control the exact colors or arrangement in the output.
- creative control：创作性控制。
  - 例句：Repeated selection, editing and compositional changes showed greater creative control over the final image.
- 口头表达：The key issue is whether the human user exercised sufficient creative control over the final expression.
- 口头表达：A prompt alone does not automatically prove human authorship; the entire creative process must be examined.

### 案例讨论常用表达

- protectable expression：可受保护的表达。
  - 例句：The court compared only protectable expression after filtering out ideas and standard elements.
- unprotectable idea：不受保护的思想。
  - 例句：The general concept of running a virtual restaurant was an unprotectable idea.
- factual information：事实信息。
  - 例句：Names and telephone numbers were factual information that anyone could reproduce.
- selection and arrangement：选择与编排。
  - 例句：The unusual selection and arrangement of historical letters showed sufficient originality.
- thin copyright：较薄的著作权保护。
  - 例句：The simple map received only thin copyright, so only very close copying would infringe.
- substantial similarity：实质性相似。
  - 例句：After filtering out the shared idea and standard elements, the court found substantial similarity in the original character design.
- 口头表达：The individual facts are not protected, but an original selection or arrangement may be protectable.
- 口头表达：The similarity concerns functional elements rather than protectable expression.

## 三、著作权法：权利内容、归属与邻接权

### 经济权利

- economic rights：著作财产权。
  - 例句：The author licensed economic rights while retaining the personal right to be named.
- right of reproduction：复制权。
  - 例句：Scanning the entire textbook and storing PDF copies implicated the right of reproduction.
  - 口头表达：The reproduction right covers the making of copies of a work.
- right of distribution：发行权。
  - 例句：Selling unauthorized printed copies to students infringed the right of distribution.
- right of rental：出租权。
  - 例句：A shop that rented software discs for a fee exercised the right of rental without permission.
- right of exhibition：展览权。
  - 例句：Displaying the original painting to the public implicated the right of exhibition.
- right of performance：表演权。
  - 例句：A restaurant's live performance of the song required permission under the right of performance.
- right of communication to the public：向公众传播权。
  - 例句：Streaming the concert to thousands of subscribers implicated the right of communication to the public.
- right of broadcasting：广播权。
  - 例句：The television station obtained the right of broadcasting before transmitting the film over the air.
- right of adaptation：改编权。
  - 例句：Turning the novel into a screenplay required authorization under the right of adaptation.
- right of translation：翻译权。
  - 例句：Publishing an English version of the Chinese novel implicated the right of translation.
- right of information network dissemination：信息网络传播权。
  - 例句：Uploading the film for users to watch at a chosen time implicated the right of information network dissemination.
  - 口头表达：The user may access the work at a time and place individually chosen by the user.
- making available to the public：向公众提供，使公众可按需获得。
  - 例句：A permanent download link amounted to making the song available to the public.
- public performance：公开表演。
  - 例句：Playing recorded music throughout a shopping mall may constitute a public performance.
- public transmission：向公众传播。
  - 例句：Sending the same program to an indeterminate audience was a public transmission.
- on-demand service：按需点播服务。
  - 例句：The platform operated an on-demand service because each subscriber selected when to watch the film.

### 精神权利

- moral rights：著作人身权。
  - 例句：Moral rights allowed the painter to object to false attribution and harmful alteration.
- right of attribution：署名权、表明作者身份的权利。
  - 例句：Removing the photographer's name from the published image interfered with the right of attribution.
- right of integrity：保护作品完整权。
  - 例句：A misleading alteration that harmed the author's reputation implicated the right of integrity.
- right of publication：发表权。
  - 例句：Releasing the unpublished manuscript without consent interfered with the author's right of publication.
- distortion：歪曲。
  - 例句：Changing a documentary to reverse its message could amount to distortion of the work.
- mutilation：篡改、割裂性修改。
  - 例句：Cutting the painting into separate commercial decorations was alleged to be mutilation.
- 口头表达：Moral rights protect the personal and reputational interests of the author.

### 权利归属和利用

- initial ownership：初始权利归属。
  - 例句：Initial ownership belonged to the individual creator before any contractual transfer was considered.
- joint work：合作作品。
  - 例句：The song was a joint work because both musicians intended their inseparable contributions to form one whole.
- joint author：合作作者。
  - 例句：Each joint author contributed original expression and shared responsibility for the completed work.
- work made for hire：雇佣作品。
  - 例句：Under the applicable common-law rule, the company claimed the software as a work made for hire.
  - 提醒：这是英美法表达，不宜机械等同于我国所有职务作品规则。
- work created in the course of employment：职务过程中创作的作品。
  - 例句：The program was a work created in the course of employment because coding it was part of the employee's assigned duties.
- commissioned work：委托作品。
  - 例句：Payment for a commissioned work did not alone decide copyright ownership when the contract was silent.
- assignment：权利转让。
  - 例句：The written assignment transferred the reproduction right from the photographer to the publisher.
- licence：许可。
  - 例句：The licence permitted streaming for two years but left ownership with the author.
  - 美式拼写：license 可同时作名词和动词；英式拼写中 licence 常作名词。
- exclusive licence：专有许可。
  - 例句：The exclusive licence allowed only one platform to stream the film in the agreed territory.
- non-exclusive licence：非专有许可。
  - 例句：A non-exclusive licence allowed the author to grant the same permission to other users.
- remuneration：报酬。
  - 例句：The performer received remuneration for the commercial broadcast of the recorded performance.
- royalty：许可费、版税。
  - 例句：The publisher paid a royalty of five percent on each authorized copy sold.
- chain of title：权利链。
  - 例句：Three signed agreements established a chain of title from the author to the present claimant.
  - 口头表达：The claimant must establish a valid chain of title.

### 邻接权

- related rights：邻接权。
  - 例句：Related rights protected the performer and record producer separately from copyright in the song.
  - 同义表达：neighboring rights。
- performer：表演者。
  - 例句：The singer was the performer even though she did not compose the underlying song.
- performance：表演。
  - 例句：The actor's live interpretation of the script constituted a protected performance.
- phonogram producer：录音制品制作者。
  - 例句：The company that financed and organized the first sound fixation was the phonogram producer.
- video recording producer：录像制品制作者。
  - 例句：The video recording producer coordinated and financed the recording of images and sound.
- broadcasting organization：广播组织。
  - 例句：The broadcasting organization objected when another service retransmitted its signal without permission.
- publisher：出版者。
  - 例句：The publisher claimed protection in the typographical layout of the printed edition.
- live performance：现场表演。
  - 例句：A secret audience recording captured the live performance without the singer's consent.
- fixation of a performance：对表演的固定。
  - 例句：Saving the concert in a digital file was a fixation of a performance.
- retransmission：转播。
  - 例句：The cable service engaged in retransmission by sending the broadcaster's program to a new audience.
- 口头表达：Related rights protect performers, producers and broadcasting organizations for their contribution to the dissemination of works.

## 四、著作权法：限制、侵权与数字环境

### 权利限制

- limitation and exception：权利限制与例外。
  - 例句：Quotation for criticism was analyzed as a limitation and exception to the author's exclusive right.
- fair use：合理使用。
  - 例句：The critic argued fair use because the short quotation supported analysis and did not replace the original market.
- statutory licence：法定许可。
  - 例句：The broadcaster relied on a statutory licence but still had to pay the legally required fee.
- compulsory licence：强制许可。
  - 例句：A compulsory licence permitted use without negotiated consent under specified statutory conditions.
- three-step test：三步检验。
  - 例句：The library exception had to satisfy the three-step test before it could justify digital copying.
  - 口头表达：An exception must be limited to certain special cases, must not conflict with the normal exploitation of the work, and must not unreasonably prejudice the legitimate interests of the right holder.
- normal exploitation：作品的正常利用。
  - 例句：Free public access conflicted with normal exploitation because it replaced the right holder's licensed service.
- legitimate interests：合法利益。
  - 例句：The exception could not unreasonably prejudice the author's legitimate interests.
- quotation：引用。
  - 例句：The review used a short quotation and identified both the author and source.
- private study：个人学习。
  - 例句：A student made one copy for private study rather than distributing it to the class.
- classroom teaching：课堂教学。
  - 例句：Limited display during classroom teaching differed from uploading the textbook to the open internet.
- news reporting：新闻报道。
  - 例句：The news program showed the artwork only as necessary for reporting the current event.
- parody：戏仿。
  - 例句：The parody borrowed recognizable features in order to mock the message of the original.
- transformative use：转换性使用。
  - 例句：The new commentary was transformative use because it gave the borrowed material a different purpose and meaning.
- market substitution：市场替代。
  - 例句：Uploading the full textbook caused market substitution by replacing ordinary student purchases.
- first-sale doctrine：首次销售原则。
  - 例句：The first-sale doctrine allowed the buyer to resell a genuine physical copy.
- exhaustion of the distribution right：发行权穷竭。
  - 例句：An authorized sale exhausted the distribution right in that particular disc, not the right to reproduce it.
  - 口头表达：Once a particular copy is lawfully sold, the distribution right in that copy is generally exhausted.

### 侵权判断

- copyright infringement：著作权侵权。
  - 例句：Posting the photographer's image in a commercial advertisement without permission was copyright infringement.
- direct infringement：直接侵权。
  - 例句：The platform committed direct infringement when it selected and uploaded the pirated film itself.
- secondary liability：间接责任、次级责任。
  - 例句：Secondary liability depended on the intermediary's knowledge, assistance and control over users' conduct.
- contributory infringement：帮助侵权、辅助侵权。
  - 例句：The operator faced contributory infringement after knowingly providing tools that materially assisted copying.
- vicarious liability：替代责任。
  - 例句：Vicarious liability was argued because the operator controlled the activity and benefited financially from it.
- access：接触作品的可能性。
  - 例句：Access was inferred from evidence that the defendant had received the unpublished manuscript.
- copying：复制行为。
  - 例句：Striking similarity plus access supported an inference of copying.
- substantial similarity：实质性相似。
  - 例句：The two works showed substantial similarity in protected dialogue and scene sequence.
- unauthorized use：未经许可的使用。
  - 例句：The licence covered print publication, so online streaming remained unauthorized use.
- defence：抗辩。
  - 例句：The defendant raised quotation for criticism as a defence to the copyright claim.
- innocent infringement：善意或不知情侵权。
  - 例句：The shop asserted innocent infringement because it reasonably believed the supplied copies were lawful.
- cease the infringement：停止侵权。
  - 例句：The court ordered the website to cease the infringement and remove the unauthorized files.
- 口头表达：The claimant normally needs to identify the protected expression, the defendant's act and the absence of authorization or a valid exception.

### 网络服务与技术措施

- internet service provider：网络服务提供者。
  - 例句：The internet service provider merely transmitted user data automatically and did not select the content.
  - 常用缩写：ISP。
- online platform：网络平台。
  - 例句：The online platform hosted videos uploaded by millions of users.
- user-generated content：用户生成内容。
  - 例句：The disputed review video was user-generated content rather than material created by the platform.
  - 常用缩写：UGC。
- notice-and-takedown procedure：通知删除程序。
  - 例句：The owner identified the exact URL through the notice-and-takedown procedure.
- safe harbor：安全港规则。
  - 例句：The provider relied on safe harbor after promptly removing the specifically identified file.
- actual knowledge：实际知道。
  - 例句：The detailed notice gave the platform actual knowledge of the particular infringement.
- constructive knowledge：应当知道、推定知道。
  - 例句：Repeated obvious warning signs supported an argument that the operator had constructive knowledge.
- duty to monitor：监控义务。
  - 例句：Safe-harbor rules did not impose a general duty to monitor every user upload.
- technological protection measure：技术保护措施。
  - 例句：DVD encryption was a technological protection measure controlling access to the film.
  - 常用缩写：TPM。
- circumvention：规避技术措施。
  - 例句：Using DeCSS to defeat the encryption was circumvention of the access control.
- anti-circumvention rule：反规避规则。
  - 例句：The anti-circumvention rule prohibited distributing a tool designed primarily to defeat effective protection.
- rights-management information：权利管理信息。
  - 例句：Deleting the photographer's name and ownership data removed rights-management information.
  - 常用缩写：RMI。
- digital rights management：数字版权管理。
  - 例句：The subscription service used digital rights management to limit copying and unauthorized playback.
  - 常用缩写：DRM。
- 口头表达：A platform may rely on a safe harbor if it responds properly after obtaining knowledge of specific infringement.
- 口头表达：Circumventing an effective technological measure may violate a separate statutory rule even where no exclusive right is directly infringed.

## 五、专利法：客体、申请与授权条件

### 基础概念

- patent：专利、专利权。
  - 例句：The patent allowed the company to prevent competitors from making the claimed medical device.
- patent law：专利法。
  - 例句：Patent law rewards technical disclosure with a limited period of exclusive protection.
- invention：发明。
  - 例句：The new filtration system was an invention because it provided a concrete technical solution.
- utility model：实用新型。
  - 例句：The improved mechanical connector was protected as a utility model rather than an invention patent.
- design patent：外观设计专利。
  - 例句：The design patent protected the product's visual appearance instead of its technical operation.
- inventor：发明人、设计人。
  - 例句：The engineer who conceived the essential technical features was named as the inventor.
- applicant：申请人。
  - 例句：The company became the applicant after the inventor assigned the right to file.
- patentee：专利权人。
  - 例句：The patentee sent a licensing offer before bringing the infringement action.
- patent application：专利申请。
  - 例句：The patent application included claims, a description, drawings and an abstract.
- patent office：专利行政部门、专利局。
  - 例句：The patent office rejected the claim after finding the invention in an earlier publication.
- technical solution：技术方案。
  - 例句：The claim described a technical solution combining a sensor, processor and control method.
- technical problem：技术问题。
  - 例句：The application identified excessive energy consumption as the technical problem.
- technical effect：技术效果。
  - 例句：Lower power use was the technical effect produced by the new circuit arrangement.
- technology：技术。
  - 例句：Patent law protects qualifying technology rather than a performer's personal technique.
- technique：技巧、技艺。
  - 例句：The chef's cutting technique was a skill, not a patentable technical solution.
  - 易混提醒：专利法中的技术方案通常用 technology 或 technical solution，不宜简单说 technique。
- claim：权利要求。
  - 例句：The claim defined the legal boundary that the accused product had to be compared against.
- specification：说明书。
  - 例句：The specification taught a skilled person how to make and use the invention.
- abstract：摘要。
  - 例句：The abstract summarized the invention but did not determine the scope of protection.
- drawing：附图。
  - 例句：The drawing showed how the claimed components were physically connected.
- filing date：申请日。
  - 例句：A journal article published before the filing date could count as prior art.
- priority date：优先权日。
  - 例句：The valid priority date allowed the applicant to rely on its earlier foreign filing.
- priority right：优先权。
  - 例句：The priority right protected the applicant against intervening filings during the priority period.
- disclosure：公开、披露。
  - 例句：The public demonstration disclosed every feature of the invention before filing.
- publication of an application：申请公开。
  - 例句：Publication of an application made the technical teaching available to the public.
- substantive examination：实质审查。
  - 例句：During substantive examination, the examiner assessed novelty, inventive step and other requirements.

### 可专利客体

- patent-eligible subject matter：可专利客体。
  - 例句：A concrete machine was patent-eligible subject matter, unlike a bare mathematical formula.
- patent eligibility：专利适格性。
  - 例句：Patent eligibility asked whether the claimed diagnostic rule belonged to a protectable statutory category.
- discovery：发现。
  - 例句：Discovering a natural correlation did not by itself create a patentable invention.
- law of nature：自然规律。
  - 例句：The claim could not monopolize a law of nature without adding an inventive technical application.
- natural phenomenon：自然现象。
  - 例句：Naturally occurring genetic information remained a natural phenomenon despite being discovered through costly research.
- abstract idea：抽象思想。
  - 例句：Using a generic computer to implement an abstract idea did not automatically make it patentable.
- diagnostic method：疾病诊断方法。
  - 例句：The diagnostic method identified disease by comparing a patient's measured values with a threshold.
- method of treatment：疾病治疗方法。
  - 例句：The method of treatment required administering a specified drug dosage to the patient.
- computer-implemented invention：计算机实施的发明。
  - 例句：The computer-implemented invention improved memory management inside the computer itself.
- graphical user interface：图形用户界面。
  - 例句：The graphical user interface was claimed together with the display product on which it appeared.
  - 常用缩写：GUI。
- genetically modified organism：转基因生物。
  - 例句：The genetically modified organism had capabilities not found in the naturally occurring bacterium.
  - 常用缩写：GMO。
- isolated gene：分离基因。
  - 例句：The isolated gene retained the same natural genetic information and was therefore challenged as ineligible.
- complementary DNA：互补DNA。
  - 例句：Complementary DNA omitted non-coding regions and did not exist in precisely that form in nature.
  - 常用缩写：cDNA。
- 口头表达：The issue is not merely whether the invention involves a natural phenomenon, but whether the claimed subject matter adds a human-made technical contribution.

### 授权实质条件

- novelty：新颖性。
  - 例句：The claim lacked novelty because one earlier patent disclosed every required element.
- inventive step：创造性。
  - 例句：The claim had an inventive step because the prior art gave no reason to make the successful combination.
  - 美国法常用：non-obviousness。
- utility：实用性。
  - 例句：The applicant showed utility by identifying a specific and credible use for the compound.
- industrial applicability：工业实用性。
  - 例句：The machine satisfied industrial applicability because it could be repeatedly manufactured and used.
- prior art：现有技术、在先技术。
  - 例句：A manual available online before filing formed part of the prior art.
- anticipation：缺乏新颖性、被现有技术完整预见。
  - 例句：Anticipation required a single earlier reference to disclose all elements of the claim.
- obviousness：显而易见性。
  - 例句：Replacing one known material with an equivalent known substitute was found obvious.
- person skilled in the art：本领域技术人员。
  - 例句：The person skilled in the art was assumed to know ordinary technical knowledge but not the invention itself.
- closest prior art：最接近的现有技术。
  - 例句：The examiner selected the most similar earlier device as the closest prior art.
- distinguishing feature：区别技术特征。
  - 例句：The low-temperature control circuit was the distinguishing feature over the earlier machine.
- technical motivation：技术启示、技术动机。
  - 例句：Nothing in the references supplied a technical motivation to combine the two separate systems.
- enablement：充分公开到能够实施。
  - 例句：Enablement failed because the specification did not teach how to achieve the claimed result.
- sufficient disclosure：充分公开。
  - 例句：Sufficient disclosure required instructions clear enough for a skilled person to carry out the invention.
- written description：书面说明要求。
  - 例句：The written description did not support the broad genus added to the amended claim.
- best mode：最佳实施方式。
  - 例句：The application disclosed the best mode known to the inventor for operating the prototype.
- grace period：不丧失新颖性的宽限期。
  - 例句：The inventor relied on the grace period after displaying the prototype shortly before filing.
- public use：公开使用。
  - 例句：Unrestricted public use of the device before filing threatened the claim's novelty.
- offer for sale：许诺销售。
  - 例句：A detailed commercial quotation constituted an offer for sale before any unit was delivered.
- 口头表达：Novelty asks whether all elements were disclosed in a single item of prior art.
- 口头表达：Inventive step asks whether the claimed solution would have been obvious to a person skilled in the art.

## 六、专利法：权利范围、限制与侵权

### 权利内容和利用

- scope of patent protection：专利保护范围。
  - 例句：The wording of the claims provided the starting point for determining the scope of patent protection.
- claim construction：权利要求解释。
  - 例句：During claim construction, the court read the disputed term in light of the description and drawings.
- independent claim：独立权利要求。
  - 例句：The independent claim contained all essential features without referring to another claim.
- dependent claim：从属权利要求。
  - 例句：The dependent claim incorporated claim 1 and added a narrower temperature limit.
- essential technical feature：必要技术特征。
  - 例句：Removing one essential technical feature placed the accused device outside literal infringement.
- patent licensing：专利许可。
  - 例句：Patent licensing allowed the manufacturer to use the invention in return for royalties.
- exclusive licence：独占许可。
  - 例句：Under the exclusive licence, only the licensee could exploit the patent within the agreed field.
- sole licence：排他许可。
  - 例句：The sole licence allowed the patentee and one licensee, but no additional licensees, to exploit the invention.
- non-exclusive licence：普通许可。
  - 例句：The non-exclusive licence permitted several competing manufacturers to use the same technology.
- standard-essential patent：标准必要专利。
  - 例句：A standard-essential patent had to be used by any manufacturer implementing the technical standard.
  - 常用缩写：SEP。
- fair, reasonable and non-discriminatory terms：公平、合理、无歧视条件。
  - 例句：The owner offered access on fair, reasonable and non-discriminatory terms to all willing implementers.
  - 常用缩写：FRAND。
- willing licensor：有许可意愿的许可人。
  - 例句：A willing licensor made a concrete written offer and explained the proposed royalty calculation.
- willing licensee：有许可意愿的被许可人。
  - 例句：A willing licensee responded promptly and provided security while negotiating disputed terms.
- patent pool：专利池。
  - 例句：The patent pool offered one package licence covering complementary patents from several owners.
- cross-licensing：交叉许可。
  - 例句：Cross-licensing allowed both companies to use each other's overlapping patent portfolios.

### 专利权限制

- exhaustion of patent rights：专利权穷竭。
  - 例句：Exhaustion of patent rights allowed resale of the machine after an authorized first sale.
- parallel importation：平行进口。
  - 例句：Parallel importation involved genuine patented goods first sold abroad and later imported without a new licence.
- prior-use right：先用权。
  - 例句：The prior-use right allowed the factory to continue the process it had already used before filing.
- experimental-use exception：实验使用例外。
  - 例句：Researchers invoked the experimental-use exception while testing how the patented compound worked.
- Bolar exception：药品和医疗器械行政审批例外。
  - 例句：The Bolar exception covered trials reasonably necessary to obtain approval for a generic drug.
- compulsory licence：强制许可。
  - 例句：A compulsory licence authorized use without consent while requiring payment of adequate remuneration.
- public health emergency：公共健康紧急状态。
  - 例句：A public health emergency strengthened the case for rapid access to essential patented medicine.
- temporary transit：临时过境。
  - 例句：Temporary transit protected patented equipment carried on a foreign vessel briefly entering the country.
- repair：维修。
  - 例句：Replacing a worn battery was repair because it preserved the identity of the original patented product.
- reconstruction：重新制造。
  - 例句：Building a functioning machine from discarded cores amounted to reconstruction rather than repair.
- 口头表达：Repair preserves the identity of the patented product, whereas reconstruction effectively creates a new product.
- 口头表达：The Bolar exception allows necessary regulatory testing before patent expiry so that a generic product may enter the market promptly afterward.

### 专利侵权

- patent infringement：专利侵权。
  - 例句：Patent infringement was established only after the court construed the claims and found every required feature in the accused device.
- literal infringement：字面侵权、相同侵权。
  - 例句：Literal infringement existed because the accused device contained every element stated in the claim.
- doctrine of equivalents：等同原则。
  - 例句：The doctrine of equivalents covered a substitute performing substantially the same function in the same way.
- all-elements rule：全面覆盖原则。
  - 例句：The all-elements rule prevented equivalence from eliminating an express claim limitation.
- prosecution history estoppel：禁止反悔原则。
  - 例句：Prosecution history estoppel barred the patentee from recapturing subject matter surrendered during examination.
- dedication to the public：捐献原则。
  - 例句：A disclosed but unclaimed alternative was treated as a dedication to the public.
- indirect infringement：间接侵权。
  - 例句：Supplying a specially adapted component with knowledge of its intended use supported indirect infringement.
- contributory infringement：帮助型专利侵权。
  - 例句：Contributory infringement was alleged because the component had no substantial non-infringing use.
- induced infringement：诱导侵权。
  - 例句：Detailed instructions encouraging customers to practise the patented method supported induced infringement.
- manufacture：制造。
  - 例句：Assembling the claimed device in the factory constituted manufacture of the patented product.
- use：使用。
  - 例句：Operating the patented machine in commercial production constituted unauthorized use.
- sale：销售。
  - 例句：Transferring the infringing device to a customer for payment was a sale.
- offer for sale：许诺销售。
  - 例句：Displaying the patented machine with a price and delivery terms at the trade fair amounted to an offer for sale.
- importation：进口。
  - 例句：Bringing infringing products into the domestic market constituted importation.
- process patent：方法专利。
  - 例句：The process patent protected a sequence of manufacturing steps rather than the resulting machine itself.
- product directly obtained by a patented process：依专利方法直接获得的产品。
  - 例句：The imported chemical was a product directly obtained by a patented process.
- design infringement：外观设计专利侵权。
  - 例句：Design infringement depended on whether the accused appearance created a similar overall visual impression.
- ordinary observer：一般消费者、普通观察者。
  - 例句：The ordinary observer compared the designs as a whole during normal product selection.
- counterfeit patent marking：假冒专利标识。
  - 例句：Printing another patent's number on an unrelated product was counterfeit patent marking.
- 口头表达：The court first construes the claims and then compares every technical feature with the accused product or process.
- 口头表达：Under the doctrine of equivalents, a substantially equivalent feature may still fall within the scope of protection, subject to limiting doctrines.

## 七、商标法：功能、注册与权利状态

### 基础概念

- trademark：商标。
  - 例句：The word and logo functioned as a trademark because they identified the commercial source of the shoes.
- service mark：服务商标。
  - 例句：The hotel used a service mark to distinguish its accommodation services from those of competitors.
- trade name：商号、企业名称。
  - 例句：The company displayed its trade name on corporate documents and a separate trademark on products.
- brand：品牌。
  - 例句：Advertising turned the product name into a valuable brand, but legal protection still depended on specific rights.
  - 易混提醒：brand 是市场营销概念，trademark 才是特定法律概念。
- sign：标志。
  - 例句：A color, sound, word or shape may serve as a sign if the public can perceive it as identifying origin.
- source identifier：来源识别标志。
  - 例句：The red label operated as a source identifier because consumers associated it with one producer.
- origin function：来源识别功能。
  - 例句：Use on the package affected the origin function by suggesting that the goods came from the trademark owner.
- quality-assurance function：质量保证功能。
  - 例句：Consistent manufacture under the same mark supported its quality-assurance function.
- advertising function：广告功能。
  - 例句：The defendant exploited the advertising function by using the famous logo to attract attention.
- investment function：投资功能。
  - 例句：Imitation interfered with the investment function by undermining the image built through long promotion.
- goodwill：商誉。
  - 例句：Years of reliable sales created goodwill that increased the value of the registered mark.
- consumer search costs：消费者搜寻成本。
  - 例句：A clear trademark lowers consumer search costs by helping buyers find the producer they trust.
- trademark owner：商标权人。
  - 例句：The trademark owner authorized the distributor to sell genuine branded goods.
- registrant：注册人。
  - 例句：The registrant had to prove genuine use to resist the cancellation request.
- trademark application：商标注册申请。
  - 例句：The trademark application listed the sign and the specific goods for which protection was sought.
- trademark registration：商标注册。
  - 例句：Trademark registration gave the owner an exclusive position for the approved sign and goods.
- registered trademark：注册商标。
  - 例句：The seller placed a sign identical to the registered trademark on counterfeit watches.
- unregistered trademark：未注册商标。
  - 例句：The claimant relied on prior use and market reputation in protecting its unregistered trademark.
- trademark office：商标主管机关。
  - 例句：The trademark office examined distinctiveness and conflicts with earlier marks.
- opposition：异议。
  - 例句：The earlier owner filed an opposition before the junior application could proceed to registration.
- invalidation：无效宣告。
  - 例句：Bad faith at the filing date supported invalidation of the trademark registration.
- cancellation：撤销。
  - 例句：Three years of unjustified non-use exposed the trademark to cancellation.
- renewal：续展。
  - 例句：Timely renewal extended the trademark registration for another protection period.

### 注册条件

- distinctiveness：显著性、识别性。
  - 例句：The unusual invented word had distinctiveness because consumers perceived it as a badge of origin.
- inherent distinctiveness：固有显著性。
  - 例句：The coined term possessed inherent distinctiveness before any market use.
- acquired distinctiveness：获得显著性。
  - 例句：Extensive advertising and sales gave the descriptive phrase acquired distinctiveness.
- secondary meaning：第二含义。
  - 例句：Survey evidence showed that the geographic term had developed a secondary meaning identifying one producer.
- generic term：通用名称。
  - 例句：No trader could monopolize the generic term used by the public as the product's common name.
- descriptive mark：描述性标志。
  - 例句：The phrase was a descriptive mark because it directly stated the refrigerator's fresh-keeping feature.
- suggestive mark：暗示性标志。
  - 例句：The suggestive mark required imagination to connect the word with the software service.
- arbitrary mark：任意性标志。
  - 例句：Using an ordinary word unrelated to computers created an arbitrary mark for computer goods.
- fanciful mark：臆造标志。
  - 例句：The invented expression had no dictionary meaning and therefore operated as a fanciful mark.
- functionality：功能性。
  - 例句：Functionality prevented trademark law from monopolizing a shape necessary for the product to work.
- technical function：技术功能。
  - 例句：The connector's shape performed a technical function by allowing the parts to lock together.
- aesthetic functionality：审美功能性。
  - 例句：Aesthetic functionality mattered because exclusive control of the attractive pattern could hinder competition.
- three-dimensional mark：三维标志。
  - 例句：The distinctive bottle was registered as a three-dimensional mark for beverages.
- shape mark：形状商标。
  - 例句：The applicant sought a shape mark covering the unusual outline of the perfume container.
- color mark：颜色商标。
  - 例句：The single color qualified as a color mark only after consumers associated it with one commercial source.
- sound mark：声音商标。
  - 例句：A short musical sequence functioned as a sound mark when listeners recognized the service provider.
- deceptive mark：欺骗性标志。
  - 例句：The label was a deceptive mark because it falsely suggested that the whisky came from Scotland.
- contrary to public order or morality：违反公共秩序或善良风俗。
  - 例句：The offensive phrase was refused as contrary to public order or morality in its social context.
- bad-faith application：恶意注册申请。
  - 例句：Filing a competitor's known sign merely to block its market entry was a bad-faith application.
- prior right：在先权利。
  - 例句：The requested sign conflicted with the artist's prior right in her personal name and portrait.
- prior mark：在先商标。
  - 例句：The examiner compared the application with a similar prior mark registered for competing goods.
- similar goods or services：类似商品或服务。
  - 例句：Clothing and retail clothing services could be treated as similar goods or services in context.
- identical mark：相同商标。
  - 例句：The defendant reproduced every visual and verbal element of the identical mark.
- similar mark：近似商标。
  - 例句：The junior sign was a similar mark because its sound and overall impression closely resembled the earlier one.
- 口头表达：A sign must be capable of distinguishing the applicant's goods or services from those of others.
- 口头表达：A descriptive sign may acquire distinctiveness through extensive use.

### 使用和权利维持

- trademark use：商标使用。
  - 例句：Placing the sign prominently on packaging was trademark use because it identified product origin.
- genuine use：真实使用。
  - 例句：Regular sales to real customers established genuine use of the registered mark.
- token use：象征性使用。
  - 例句：One artificial shipment arranged only to preserve registration was merely token use.
- commercial use：商业使用。
  - 例句：Offering branded goods through an online shop was commercial use in the course of trade.
- use in the course of trade：在商业活动中使用。
  - 例句：A private reference was not use in the course of trade because it had no commercial purpose.
- use as a trademark：商标性使用。
  - 例句：The word was not use as a trademark when buyers understood it only as ingredient information.
- non-use cancellation：因连续不使用而撤销。
  - 例句：The competitor sought non-use cancellation after the registrant stopped genuine sales for the statutory period.
- assignment of a trademark：商标转让。
  - 例句：The assignment of a trademark transferred ownership to the buyer under the written agreement.
- trademark licence：商标许可。
  - 例句：The trademark licence allowed the distributor to use the sign on approved goods.
- quality control：质量控制。
  - 例句：The owner exercised quality control by inspecting goods produced by the licensee.
- 口头表达：The decisive question is whether the sign is used to indicate the commercial origin of goods or services.

## 八、商标法：混淆、抗辩与驰名商标

### 商标侵权与混淆

- trademark infringement：商标侵权。
  - 例句：Using a confusingly similar logo on competing shoes supported a claim of trademark infringement.
- likelihood of confusion：混淆可能性。
  - 例句：Likelihood of confusion existed because ordinary buyers could believe the two products came from related companies.
- actual confusion：实际混淆。
  - 例句：Misaddressed orders and customer complaints provided evidence of actual confusion.
- source confusion：来源混淆。
  - 例句：Source confusion occurred when buyers believed the defendant had manufactured the goods.
- initial-interest confusion：初始兴趣混淆。
  - 例句：A misleading keyword advertisement created initial-interest confusion before users reached the correct website.
- post-sale confusion：售后混淆。
  - 例句：Post-sale confusion arose when observers mistook a counterfeit bag for a genuine luxury product.
- reverse confusion：反向混淆。
  - 例句：Reverse confusion occurred when a large newcomer overwhelmed the identity of a smaller senior user.
- affiliation：关联关系。
  - 例句：The common branding suggested an affiliation between two legally separate businesses.
- sponsorship：赞助关系。
  - 例句：Product placement raised the question whether viewers would infer sponsorship by the trademark owner.
- authorization：授权关系。
  - 例句：The words “official partner” falsely suggested authorization from the brand owner.
- similarity of marks：标志近似性。
  - 例句：Similarity of marks was assessed by comparing appearance, sound and meaning as a whole.
- similarity of goods or services：商品或服务类似性。
  - 例句：The court compared purpose, users and sales channels when assessing similarity of goods or services.
- relevant public：相关公众。
  - 例句：For specialist medical equipment, the relevant public consisted mainly of trained professional buyers.
- average consumer：普通消费者。
  - 例句：The average consumer normally retained an imperfect overall memory of the mark.
- degree of attention：注意程度。
  - 例句：The high price of industrial machinery increased the buyer's degree of attention.
- strength of the mark：商标显著性和知名度强度。
  - 例句：Long use and inherent uniqueness increased the strength of the mark.
- visual similarity：视觉近似。
  - 例句：The matching silhouette and color arrangement created strong visual similarity.
- phonetic similarity：读音近似。
  - 例句：Although spelled differently, the two words produced substantial phonetic similarity when spoken.
- conceptual similarity：含义近似。
  - 例句：Both signs referred to the same mythical animal, creating conceptual similarity.
- overall impression：整体印象。
  - 例句：Small lettering differences did not change the same overall impression produced by the two logos.
- double identity：商标和商品双重相同。
  - 例句：Double identity existed because an identical sign was used on identical goods.
- presumption of confusion：混淆推定。
  - 例句：The double-identity situation triggered a presumption of confusion that the defendant tried to rebut.
- keyword advertising：关键词广告。
  - 例句：The advertiser purchased a competitor's mark as keyword advertising to display a sponsored link.
- domain name：域名。
  - 例句：The misleading domain name diverted users searching for the trademark owner's official store.
- counterfeit goods：假冒商品。
  - 例句：The seized watches were counterfeit goods bearing unauthorized copies of the registered mark.
- intermediary liability：中介主体责任。
  - 例句：Intermediary liability depended on the platform's knowledge, control and response to identified infringements.
- 口头表达：Likelihood of confusion is assessed globally from the perspective of the relevant public.
- 口头表达：The court should consider visual, phonetic and conceptual similarity together with the similarity of the goods and the strength of the earlier mark.

### 不侵权使用与抗辩

- descriptive use：描述性使用。
  - 例句：The repairer made descriptive use of the mark when truthfully stating which cars it serviced.
- nominative use：指示性使用。
  - 例句：Nominative use allowed the reseller to name the genuine product when no practical substitute existed.
- referential use：指称性使用。
  - 例句：A news article made referential use of the mark to identify the company being discussed.
- prior use：在先使用。
  - 例句：Prior use allowed the local business to continue within its established earlier scope.
- honest practices in industrial or commercial matters：符合工商业诚信惯例。
  - 例句：The truthful description complied with honest practices in industrial or commercial matters and did not imply affiliation.
- necessary use：必要使用。
  - 例句：Naming the printer model was necessary use because customers needed compatibility information.
- comparative advertising：比较广告。
  - 例句：The comparative advertising named a competitor but accurately compared verifiable product features.
- resale of genuine goods：真品转售。
  - 例句：Resale of genuine goods normally did not imply that the reseller was an authorized dealer.
- exhaustion of trademark rights：商标权穷竭。
  - 例句：The authorized first sale triggered exhaustion of trademark rights in that particular item.
- repackaging：重新包装。
  - 例句：Repackaging defeated exhaustion where it damaged the product and harmed the mark's reputation.
- parody：戏仿。
  - 例句：The dog toy used obvious parody to evoke the luxury handbag while signaling a joke.
- freedom of expression：表达自由。
  - 例句：Freedom of expression supported non-commercial criticism that merely referred to the famous film title.
- non-commercial use：非商业使用。
  - 例句：A private fan's discussion of the logo was non-commercial use rather than source-identifying trade.
- abuse of rights：权利滥用。
  - 例句：The court found abuse of rights where a bad-faith registrant sued only to suppress the earlier legitimate user.
- 口头表达：The defendant uses the mark only to describe the characteristics of the product, not to identify commercial origin.
- 口头表达：Nominative use may be permissible when reference to the trademark is reasonably necessary and does not imply sponsorship or authorization.

### 驰名商标保护

- well-known trademark：驰名商标。
  - 例句：The toy logo qualified as a well-known trademark because a large part of the relevant public recognized it.
  - 易错提醒：不要把驰名商标说成 famous brand；法律术语优先用 well-known trademark。
- recognition by the relevant public：相关公众的知晓程度。
  - 例句：Surveys, sales and advertising evidence established recognition by the relevant public.
- protection across dissimilar goods：跨不类似商品保护。
  - 例句：Exceptional reputation justified protection across dissimilar goods when association and injury were shown.
- dilution：淡化。
  - 例句：Repeated use on unrelated products threatened dilution even without ordinary source confusion.
- dilution by blurring：弱化型淡化。
  - 例句：Dilution by blurring weakened the unique link between the famous mark and one source.
- dilution by tarnishment：丑化型淡化。
  - 例句：Use on obscene low-quality goods created dilution by tarnishment of the respected mark.
- free-riding：搭便车。
  - 例句：The junior user engaged in free-riding by borrowing the famous mark's attraction without investment.
- unfair advantage：不正当利用。
  - 例句：The copied imagery gave the new product an unfair advantage derived from the earlier mark's reputation.
- detriment to distinctive character：损害显著性。
  - 例句：Widespread imitation caused detriment to distinctive character by weakening the mark's uniqueness.
- detriment to reputation：损害商誉。
  - 例句：Association with dangerous products created detriment to reputation for the earlier mark.
- association：联想。
  - 例句：Consumers formed an association with the famous mark even though they did not believe the goods shared a source.
- family of marks：商标系列、联合商标家族。
  - 例句：Repeated use of the same prefix with food names created a recognizable family of marks.
- case-by-case recognition：个案认定。
  - 例句：Case-by-case recognition limited well-known status to the concrete dispute and evidence presented.
- passive protection：被动保护。
  - 例句：Passive protection meant that the authority recognized the well-known mark only when a party requested relief.
- protection on demand：按需认定和保护。
  - 例句：Protection on demand prevented the creation of a permanent administrative list of famous marks.
- 口头表达：Protection against dilution does not always require confusion, but it normally requires a mental association with the well-known mark.
- 口头表达：The claimant must show unfair advantage or detriment rather than relying on reputation alone.

## 九、侵权责任与救济

### 民事救济

- civil liability：民事责任。
  - 例句：Civil liability required the infringer to stop the unlawful sale and compensate the proven loss.
- remedy：救济。
  - 例句：The claimant requested a remedy tailored to the continuing online infringement.
- injunction：禁令。
  - 例句：The injunction ordered the manufacturer to stop producing the infringing device.
- preliminary injunction：行为保全、临时禁令。
  - 例句：A preliminary injunction prevented market launch while the urgent patent dispute was pending.
- permanent injunction：永久禁令。
  - 例句：After the final finding of infringement, a permanent injunction barred future sales.
- damages：损害赔偿。
  - 例句：The court awarded damages for the commercial loss caused by counterfeit products.
- actual loss：实际损失。
  - 例句：Falling sales supported actual loss only after other market causes were excluded.
- profits of the infringer：侵权人获利。
  - 例句：The defendant's accounts showed the profits of the infringer attributable to unauthorized sales.
- reasonable royalty：合理许可费。
  - 例句：A comparable licence helped the court estimate a reasonable royalty for the unauthorized patent use.
- statutory damages：法定赔偿。
  - 例句：The court used statutory damages because neither actual loss nor unlawful profit could be reliably calculated.
- punitive damages：惩罚性赔偿。
  - 例句：Deliberate and serious counterfeiting justified consideration of punitive damages.
- reasonable expenses：合理开支。
  - 例句：Investigation fees and necessary lawyer costs were claimed as reasonable expenses.
- burden of proof：举证责任。
  - 例句：The burden of proof initially required the claimant to establish ownership and the accused act.
- evidence preservation：证据保全。
  - 例句：Evidence preservation secured warehouse records that might otherwise have been destroyed.
- destruction of infringing goods：销毁侵权商品。
  - 例句：Destruction of infringing goods prevented counterfeit stock from returning to the market.
- cessation of infringement：停止侵害。
  - 例句：Removing the unauthorized files achieved cessation of infringement.
- irreparable harm：不可弥补的损害。
  - 例句：Loss of market exclusivity before product launch supported a finding of irreparable harm.
- balance of hardships：困难或利益衡量。
  - 例句：The balance of hardships favored the claimant because stopping deliberate copying imposed no legitimate loss.
- 口头表达：The court may grant an injunction and award damages, depending on the nature and seriousness of the infringement.
- 口头表达：Punitive damages normally require intentional and serious infringement.

### 行政与刑事责任

- administrative enforcement：行政执法。
  - 例句：Market regulators used administrative enforcement to inspect the shop and seize fake goods.
- administrative penalty：行政处罚。
  - 例句：The authority imposed an administrative penalty for selling goods with counterfeit marks.
- confiscation：没收。
  - 例句：Confiscation removed both the illegal stock and the proceeds obtained from its sale.
- fine：罚款。
  - 例句：The regulator calculated the fine by considering sales volume and seriousness.
- criminal liability：刑事责任。
  - 例句：Large-scale intentional counterfeiting could trigger criminal liability in addition to civil claims.
- intentional infringement：故意侵权。
  - 例句：Internal messages showed intentional infringement rather than an innocent mistake.
- serious circumstances：情节严重。
  - 例句：High sales, long duration and repeated violations supported a finding of serious circumstances.
- 口头表达：The same conduct may give rise to civil, administrative or criminal consequences under different conditions.

## 十、口试答题连接词

### 引出问题

- The key issue is whether ...：关键问题在于是否……
  - 例句：The key issue is whether the disputed interface contains original expression or only functional elements.
- The first question is ...：首先要判断……
  - 例句：The first question is whether the claimant owns a valid and enforceable right.
- This case concerns ...：本案涉及……
  - 例句：This case concerns the unauthorized online transmission of a protected film.
- The dispute centers on ...：争议集中于……
  - 例句：The dispute centers on whether consumers are likely to believe that the two businesses are connected.
- There are two separate issues here.：这里有两个需要分别处理的问题。
  - 例句：There are two separate issues here: whether the patent is valid and whether the accused product falls within its claims.

### 陈述规则

- As a general rule, ...：一般而言……
  - 例句：As a general rule, a descriptive sign cannot be registered without acquired distinctiveness.
- The basic rule is that ...：基本规则是……
  - 例句：The basic rule is that copyright protects original expression rather than ideas.
- To establish infringement, the claimant must show ...：要成立侵权，原告必须证明……
  - 例句：To establish infringement, the claimant must show ownership of a valid right and an unauthorized act within its scope.
- The relevant factors include ...：相关考虑因素包括……
  - 例句：The relevant factors include the similarity of the marks, the goods and the degree of consumer attention.
- This requirement serves to ...：该项要求旨在……
  - 例句：This requirement serves to keep abstract ideas and natural laws available to the public.
- The rule is subject to an exception.：该规则存在例外。
  - 例句：The rule is subject to an exception for certain acts carried out solely for regulatory approval.

### 分析事实

- In the present case, ...：在本案中……
  - 例句：In the present case, the defendant reproduced the entire photograph in an online advertisement.
- On these facts, ...：根据这些事实……
  - 例句：On these facts, the seller knew that the goods carried counterfeit marks.
- This suggests that ...：这表明……
  - 例句：This suggests that the platform had actual knowledge of the specific infringement.
- The stronger argument is that ...：更有说服力的观点是……
  - 例句：The stronger argument is that the sign is descriptive because consumers understand it as product information.
- A court would likely consider ...：法院很可能会考虑……
  - 例句：A court would likely consider whether the accused feature performs substantially the same function in the same way.
- The fact that ... is relevant but not decisive.：……这一事实有关联，但并非决定性。
  - 例句：The fact that the marks share one word is relevant but not decisive.
- The analysis depends on ...：判断取决于……
  - 例句：The analysis depends on how the relevant public perceives the sign in its commercial context.

### 表达争议

- One view is that ..., while the opposing view is that ...：一种观点认为……，相反观点认为……
  - 例句：One view is that the interface reflects creative choices, while the opposing view is that its layout is dictated by function.
- The controversy arises because ...：争议产生于……
  - 例句：The controversy arises because broad protection may restrict legitimate competition and follow-on innovation.
- This interpretation favors ..., whereas the alternative gives greater weight to ...：这一解释偏重……，另一解释则更重视……
  - 例句：This interpretation favors the patent owner, whereas the alternative gives greater weight to market access.
- The better view may be ... because ...：更可取的观点可能是……，理由在于……
  - 例句：The better view may be to examine consumer perception because trademark law protects source identification.
- The issue should be decided on a case-by-case basis.：该问题应当个案判断。
  - 例句：The issue should be decided on a case-by-case basis because the commercial context may change consumer perception.

### 给出结论

- Therefore, the conduct is likely to constitute infringement.：因此，该行为很可能构成侵权。
  - 例句：Therefore, the conduct is likely to constitute infringement because every element of the patent claim is present.
- Therefore, the defence is likely to succeed.：因此，该抗辩很可能成立。
  - 例句：Therefore, the defence is likely to succeed because the mark is used only to describe compatibility.
- On balance, ...：综合而言……
  - 例句：On balance, the public interest in access does not outweigh the right holder's normal market in this case.
- The conclusion may change if ...：如果……，结论可能发生变化。
  - 例句：The conclusion may change if the defendant proves that the copy was lawfully acquired and resold.
- A definite conclusion requires further facts about ...：要作出确定结论，还需要了解……方面的事实。
  - 例句：A definite conclusion requires further facts about the defendant's knowledge and control over the infringing activity.

## 十一、易混词辨析

### right、ownership与title

- right：具体权利。
  - 例句：The reproduction right was only one specific legal power within the copyright bundle.
- ownership：权利归属状态。
  - 例句：Ownership answered who held the copyright after the written transfer.
- title：权源或完整权利链。
  - 例句：The claimant established title through an unbroken chain of signed assignments.
- 例句：The claimant owns the copyright and has established a valid chain of title.

### licence与assignment

- licence：许可他人使用，权利本身通常仍归许可人。
  - 例句：The licence permitted online use but left ownership with the author.
- assignment：全部或部分权利发生转让。
  - 例句：The assignment transferred the distribution right permanently to the publisher.
- 例句：A licence permits use, while an assignment transfers ownership of the right.

### invalidation、revocation与cancellation

- invalidation：因权利成立时存在瑕疵而宣告无效。
  - 例句：Invalidation treated the patent as defective because it lacked novelty when granted.
- revocation：撤销或终止权利，具体含义依制度和语境而定。
  - 例句：Revocation ended the right after the competent authority found a statutory ground.
- cancellation：商标等权利因不使用或其他法定原因被撤销、注销。
  - 例句：Cancellation removed the trademark after the owner failed to prove genuine use.
- 口试中应结合具体权利类型说明，避免互换使用。

### similarity与confusion

- similarity：标志、作品或技术特征之间的相似。
  - 例句：The marks had visual similarity because they shared the same dominant device.
- confusion：商标法中相关公众对来源、关联或授权产生误认的可能性。
  - 例句：Confusion arose when buyers believed the similar packages came from the same company.
- 例句：Similarity is an important factor, but it does not automatically establish likelihood of confusion.

### novelty与inventive step

- novelty：是否已被单一现有技术完整公开。
  - 例句：Novelty existed because no single prior reference disclosed every claim element.
- inventive step：对本领域技术人员而言是否显而易见。
  - 例句：The claim lacked an inventive step because a skilled person would readily combine the known features.
- 例句：A claim may be novel but still lack an inventive step.

### idea与expression

- idea：思想、原理、方法。
  - 例句：The idea of a school for young magicians remained free for other authors to use.
- expression：思想的具体表达。
  - 例句：The author's particular characters, dialogue and scene sequence were protected expression.
- 例句：Copyright does not protect the idea itself, but it may protect the author's original expression of that idea.

### fair use与fair dealing

- fair use：通常指开放式、因素衡量型合理使用制度。
  - 例句：Fair use applied an open-ended balance of purpose, amount and market effect.
- fair dealing：通常指限定目的下的公平使用制度。
  - 例句：Fair dealing was available only for the specific purposes listed by the statute.
- 我国法问题应优先表述为 copyright limitation or statutory exception，除非题目明确要求比较法。

### infringement与counterfeiting

- infringement：侵权的上位概念。
  - 例句：Unauthorized use of a protected sign was infringement even if the goods were not literal counterfeits.
- counterfeiting：假冒，通常强调未经许可复制标志或冒充权利状态。
  - 例句：Placing an identical luxury mark on fake bags was counterfeiting.
- 例句：Every act of counterfeiting is an infringement, but not every infringement is counterfeiting.

### well-known trademark与famous brand

- well-known trademark：驰名商标的规范法律表达。
  - 例句：The court recognized the toy logo as a well-known trademark after examining evidence of public recognition.
- famous brand：知名品牌的普通商业表达，不当然具有驰名商标法律地位。
  - 例句：The product was a famous brand in advertising terms, but that label alone did not prove legal well-known status.

## 十二、两分钟口试答题模板

### 通用模板

- Issue：The key issue is whether the defendant's conduct falls within the scope of the claimant's IP right.
  - 例句：This case concerns whether the platform should be liable for users' infringing uploads.
- Rule：As a general rule, the claimant must establish a valid right, a legally controlled act and the absence of authorization or a valid defence.
  - 例句：As a general rule, a claimant must prove a valid right and an unauthorized act within its scope.
- Analysis：In the present case, the relevant factors include the protected subject matter, the scope of the right, the nature of the defendant's conduct and its effect on the market or the relevant public.
  - 例句：Here, the defendant copied the protected photograph and used it in a commercial advertisement.
- Counterargument：The defendant may argue that the use falls within a statutory limitation or does not perform the legally protected function.
  - 例句：The defendant may argue that the quotation was limited, necessary and properly attributed.
- Conclusion：On balance, the conduct is likely or unlikely to constitute infringement, although the conclusion may depend on further facts.
  - 例句：On balance, the use is likely to infringe because it substitutes for the licensed market.

### 著作权题模板

- First, identify the work and separate protectable expression from unprotectable ideas, facts and functional elements.
- Second, identify the exclusive right allegedly affected.
- Third, examine copying, access and substantial similarity where relevant.
- Finally, consider authorization, fair use or another statutory exception.

### 专利题模板

- First, determine whether the patent is valid and in force.
- Second, construe the claims and identify every required technical feature.
- Third, compare those features with the accused product or process.
- Finally, consider literal infringement, equivalents and any statutory limitation.

### 商标题模板

- First, ask whether the sign is used in the course of trade and as a source identifier.
- Second, compare the marks and the relevant goods or services.
- Third, assess likelihood of confusion from the perspective of the relevant public.
- Finally, consider descriptive use, nominative use, prior use, exhaustion or another defence.
